# Oneul Chinese (오늘 중국어) word list

The HSK level 1 to 4 word list (1,200 words) shipped in **Oneul Chinese**, a Mandarin speaking app for Korean speakers
by Vivory Lab (iPhone, app ID `app.vivory.vocab`). The app ships this file as a separate resource file next to the app
binary. It is published here so that anyone can get, read, change and share it under its license, without the App
Store.

| Version | Shipped in | File |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1.0 | Oneul Chinese 1.1.0 (30) for iPhone, onward | [`1.1.0/zh-words.json`](1.1.0/zh-words.json) · [`SHA256SUMS.txt`](1.1.0/SHA256SUMS.txt) |

Each entry is `{"id", "lv", "hz", "py", "ko"}` = id, HSK 2.0 level, simplified Chinese, pinyin, Korean meaning.

## Source, license and what we changed

- The Chinese headwords and pinyin come from a word list derived from [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict)
  (MDBG), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](../licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Words are grouped by HSK 2.0 level.
- The Korean meanings were written by Vivory Lab for Korean learners.
- The whole file, as an adaptation of CC BY-SA material, is released under [CC BY-SA 4.0](../licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).
  Please keep the attribution when you share it.

HSK is used only to describe the level. This list is not an official list and is not affiliated with any exam provider.

The app's other material is not in this folder: the course sentences were written by Vivory Lab, the audio is
AI-generated, and the stroke-order data comes from Make Me a Hanzi under the Arphic Public License, whose full text
ships inside the app.
